# PocketWise — Solution

## 1. Problem Statement

Students often receive a fixed amount of pocket money every month, but small daily expenses such as food, travel, shopping, entertainment, and other needs can quickly add up.

By the end of the month, students may not remember:

- Where their money was spent
- Which category consumed the most money
- How much money they have already spent
- How much money is still available
- Whether they are spending more than they should

The main problem is not only spending money, but understanding spending habits.

---

## 2. Proposed Solution

**PocketWise** is a simple, student-focused expense tracking web application that helps students record, monitor, and understand their monthly spending.

Instead of only showing a list of transactions, PocketWise provides a visual overview of spending so students can quickly understand where their pocket money is going.

The application allows users to:

1. Set a monthly budget.
2. Add daily expenses.
3. Categorize expenses.
4. Track total spending.
5. View remaining budget.
6. See category-wise spending.
7. View recent transactions.
8. Get simple spending insights.

---

## 3. Target Users

PocketWise is primarily designed for:

- College students
- School students
- Students living away from home
- Young people managing a limited monthly budget

---

## 4. Key Features

### Expense Tracking

Users can add an expense by entering:

- Amount
- Category
- Description
- Date

### Budget Tracking

The dashboard shows:

- Monthly budget
- Total amount spent
- Remaining amount
- Percentage of budget used

### Spending Breakdown

Expenses are grouped into categories such as:

- Food
- Travel
- Shopping
- Entertainment
- Education
- Bills
- Other

A visual spending chart helps users quickly identify where most of their money is going.

### Recent Expenses

Users can view their latest transactions in one place.

### Smart Spending Insights

PocketWise analyzes the user's spending and provides simple, understandable insights.

For example:

> "You have spent a large portion of your budget on Food."

or:

> "Only a small portion of your monthly budget remains."

These insights are based on the user's recorded expenses and budget.

---

## 5. How PocketWise Solves the Problem

The basic flow is:

**Set Budget → Add Expenses → Categorize Spending → View Dashboard → Understand Spending → Make Better Decisions**

Instead of waiting until the end of the month to realize that money has been spent, students can monitor their spending throughout the month.

---

## 6. Core Value

PocketWise focuses on one simple question:

> **"Where did my money go?"**

The application turns individual daily expenses into an easy-to-understand overview of the student's financial habits.

This helps students become more aware of their spending and make better decisions with their limited pocket money.

---

## 7. Technology

The project is built using:

- React.js
- JavaScript
- HTML
- CSS
- Vite
- Browser LocalStorage

LocalStorage allows expense and budget information to remain available in the browser without requiring a separate backend for the MVP.

---

## 8. MVP Scope

To keep the solution practical within the hackathon time limit, the first version focuses on the core problem.

### Included

- Monthly budget
- Add expense
- Expense categories
- Expense history
- Total spending
- Remaining budget
- Spending breakdown
- Basic spending insights
- Responsive interface

### Not Included in MVP

- Bank account integration
- Online payments
- Complex authentication
- External financial APIs
- Advanced AI financial advice

These features can be considered for future development.

---

## 9. Expected Outcome

PocketWise aims to make expense tracking simple enough for a student to use every day.

By providing a clear dashboard and understandable spending insights, the application helps students:

- Know where their money goes
- Identify high-spending categories
- Monitor their monthly budget
- Avoid unnecessary overspending
- Develop better money-management habits

---

## 10. One-Line Solution

**PocketWise helps students understand where their money goes before their money disappears.**
