# PocketWise — Introduction

## About PocketWise

**PocketWise** is a student-focused expense tracking web application designed to make personal money management simple and understandable.

Students often receive a limited amount of pocket money each month. However, frequent small expenses such as food, travel, shopping, entertainment, and other daily needs can make it difficult to understand where their money has gone.

PocketWise provides a simple dashboard where students can record their expenses, monitor their monthly budget, and understand their spending patterns.

---

## Why PocketWise?

Traditional expense tracking can feel complicated or time-consuming. Students need a solution that is:

- Simple to use
- Quick for recording daily expenses
- Easy to understand
- Focused on their monthly pocket-money budget
- Helpful in identifying unnecessary spending

PocketWise focuses on these requirements through a clean and student-friendly interface.

---

## What PocketWise Provides

PocketWise allows users to:

- Set a monthly budget
- Add and categorize expenses
- Track total spending
- Monitor remaining money
- View category-wise spending
- Check recent transactions
- Understand spending patterns through visual charts
- Receive simple spending insights

---

## Project Objective

The main objective of PocketWise is to help students become more aware of their spending habits.

Instead of only recording expenses, PocketWise converts spending data into meaningful information that students can understand at a glance.

The core idea is:

> **Track your spending. Understand your habits. Make better decisions.**

---

## Target Audience

PocketWise is primarily designed for:

- College students
- School students
- Students living away from home
- Young users managing limited monthly budgets

---

## Technology Used

The current MVP is developed using:

- **React.js** — User interface
- **JavaScript** — Application logic
- **HTML** — Structure
- **CSS** — Styling and responsive design
- **Vite** — Development and build tool
- **LocalStorage** — Browser-based data persistence

---

## Vision

PocketWise aims to make financial awareness accessible to students without requiring complicated financial tools.

The long-term vision is to evolve PocketWise from a basic expense tracker into a personal financial-awareness platform that helps young users develop better money-management habits.

---

## Conclusion

PocketWise addresses a simple but common student problem:

> **"Where did my money go?"**

By combining expense tracking, budgeting, visual spending breakdowns, and simple insights, PocketWise helps students understand their money before the month ends.
